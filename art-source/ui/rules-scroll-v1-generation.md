## 完整提示词

Create ONE small game UI inventory icon: a readable unrolled parchment scroll, upright, frontal orthographic view. True chunky pixel art, like a 32 by 32 or 48 by 48 sprite enlarged with nearest-neighbor. Hard square pixels, no antialiasing, no blur, no painted gradients. Two short rolled wooden-brown ends at top and bottom; warm desaturated pale olive parchment (#d8d1ae), dark olive-brown outline (#333a2a), subtle 2-tone creases and several dark short horizontal strokes suggesting rules text (NO actual letters, NO words, NO numbers). Restrained weathered mechanical arcade game palette. Symmetrical clear silhouette, slight pixel chips along parchment edges. Center icon, occupy about 82 percent of square canvas. Transparent background with clean alpha, no backdrop, no shadow outside icon, no frame, no badges, no other objects. This will be displayed 32 logical pixels wide in top right of a portrait game beside old wooden/metal panels; legibility at tiny size is critical.
